# Known issues

Ranked by how much they would bother a player.

1. **Feel values are untuned by hand.** Camera offsets, sensitivities, recoil and move speeds use the spec's starting values and have only been checked by tests and screenshots, never played. Use the Tuning tab (Backquote) and paste the result into `src/data/tuning.ts`.
2. **Mission pacing is unverified for humans.** The loop bot (god mode, infinite ammo) finishes Landfall in about 3 simulated minutes and Cold Seam in about 2. A human should take longer, but the 8 to 12 minute target per mission and 20 to 30 minutes overall have not been measured with a real player.
3. **Animation is procedural and simple.** Poses blend between keyed angles, so limbs can look stiff, hands do not grip the weapon exactly, and roll, knockdown and death poses are basic. To save draw calls, hands and feet are merged into the forearms and shins, so they no longer bend at the wrist or ankle.
4. **Distant enemies use a frozen pose.** Beyond 18 m an enemy swaps to a single baked mesh in its idle pose to stay within the draw-call budget. It still moves and turns, but its limbs do not animate.
5. **Snow falls inside Korr's roofed buildings.** The snow particles follow the camera and are not occluded by roofs.
6. **Dialogue shots can be cramped.** The over-the-shoulder dialogue camera is placed relative to the speaker, so near walls or consoles it can frame the commander's head large in the foreground.
7. **Map ship trail is a simple cone.** It reads as an engine glow but is not a true particle trail.
8. **Synthesized audio is functional, not polished.** Every sound is generated by Web Audio recipes behind one table in `src/data/sounds.ts`, so recorded audio can replace any sound without touching game code.
9. **A shader produces an occasional NaN pixel in some views (source not identified).** With bloom on, the bloom blur used to spread it into a fully black frame (seen while harvesting near Landfall Station). A guard pass now replaces non-finite pixels before bloom, so the frame renders. The root cause is still open, likely a degenerate flat-shaded triangle.
10. **Headless test rendering uses SwiftShader.** Screenshots and draw-call counts come from software WebGL. Real GPU frame rate was not measured, by design (spec section 13).
